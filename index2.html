📖 CONTROLS & WATER POLO RULES

🏊‍♂️ MOVEMENT & BALL CONTROL

• WASD / Arrow Keys: Swim in any direction. Hold SHIFT to Sprint.

• Left-Click (Hold & Release): Charge shot power and fire toward the goal.

• E Key (With Ball): Pass directly to the nearest teammate you are facing.

• C Key: Call for a pass from your ball-carrying teammate.

• F Key: High arc Lob Pass over defenders to an open teammate.

• Q Key: Switch control to the field player closest to the ball.

🛡️ DEFENSE & TACKLE CLASH

• Left-Click / E (Off Ball): Lunge to tackle an opponent holding the ball (3s cooldown).

• B Key: Jump high out of the water to Block incoming shots or passes.

• Tackle Clash: When you tackle or get tackled, rapidly tap SPACE for 3 seconds to win possession and gain a 3-second tackle shield!
